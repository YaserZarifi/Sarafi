Privacy policy

This policy covers the offline and ad-supported editions of Sarafi for Android.

Data stored on your device

Sarafi stores cash counts and totals, exchange drafts and rates, saved records, notes, and settings in app-private storage. Sarafi can be used without an account. Financial data is excluded from automatic Android backup and device transfers.

Local data stays on your device until you delete it, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall Sarafi. You can delete saved records from the history screen. If you copy or share content, Android’s clipboard or the receiving app handles the content you select.

Live exchange rates

The ad-supported edition fetches public exchange rates over HTTPS from Sarafi’s rate service hosted on Cloudflare Workers. The app and its home-screen widgets can make these requests to refresh rates. Cloudflare processes connection information such as your IP address to deliver and protect the service. Rate requests do not include your cash counts, conversion amounts, notes, or saved records.

Advertising and consent

The offline edition has no advertising SDK or internet permission. The ad-supported edition uses Google AdMob, which may collect and share IP addresses, which can indicate approximate location, device and account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and App Set ID, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information. Google uses this data for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention and transmits it over encrypted connections. Sarafi does not send financial amounts, quantities, notes, or history to the advertising SDK.

Google’s consent tools can store privacy choices on your device. Where required, open Settings → Advertising privacy options to review or change them. You can also manage or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Google handles retention and deletion of the data it collects under its own policies.
